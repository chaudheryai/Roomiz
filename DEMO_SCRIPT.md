# Two-minute investor demo script

1. **Intro (15 seconds)** — “Roomiz is a concierge roommate-finding concept. Students can try the free questionnaire, then pay for our team to perform the search and coordinate introductions.”
2. **Questionnaire (25 seconds)** — Open the Interactive Prototype. Set housing to **UCR residence hall**, choose bedtime, cleanliness, noise and top priorities. Explain that the actual service would use this information to guide candidate sourcing.
3. **Candidate shortlist (30 seconds)** — Click **Find my demo matches**. Show 3–5 fictional candidate cards, sample fit scores, and preference explanations. “The sample data illustrates our proposed shortlist experience; no identities are verified in this demo.”
4. **Introduction request (20 seconds)** — Click **Request intro** on a sample card. Explain a future concierge would check real availability and consent before contacting anyone.
5. **Admin preview (20 seconds)** — Open **Demo admin**. Show the request and change its mock status. This demonstrates an operational workflow, not a production backend.
6. **Packages (10 seconds)** — Point out the **$59.99 Roommate Match** and **$119.99 Move-In Assistance** proposals, and that the free questionnaire is the acquisition funnel.

**If asked:** “Is this real matchmaking?” → “Not yet; this is a simulated MVP and we have not launched real onboarding, verification, payments or actual introductions. The pilot will test those processes.”
